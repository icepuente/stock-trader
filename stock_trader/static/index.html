IB Gateway

checking…
The gateway starts automatically with the server when IBKR is the provider, but logging in stays manual (IBKR requires it): in its window pick IB API, log in with your paper username (DU…), then under Configure → Settings → API → Settings tick Enable ActiveX and Socket Clients and untick Read-Only API.
  • This strategy is unvalidated on real market data — it may simply lose money.
  • Unattended market orders on your real IBKR account, up to 3 symbols at once.
  • Only takes effect once IB Gateway is logged into your live account (U…) — a paper login keeps trading paper.
  • Starting the bot will ask you to type LIVE again, every time.
The daily-loss halt flattens everything and stops the bot for the day. Saving with the box newly ticked asks you to type LIVE — enforced by the server, and Telegram can never toggle this.
